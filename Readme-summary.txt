================================================================================
HITTITE CIVILIZATION - ABILITIES SUMMARY
================================================================================

CIVILIZATION ABILITY: ARINNITTA'S EMBRACE
  • Resources have +1 Gold
  • Buildings and Improvements have +1 Gold adjacency with Resources
  • +30% Production towards constructing Yazilikaya

================================================================================
UNIQUE UNITS
================================================================================

GOLDEN SPEARMAN (Infantry Unit)
  • +3 Combat Strength vs Independents
  • Tier 2 and Tier 3 variants available
  • Culture Tree Bonus: +2 Combat Strength in Urban Districts

GOLDEN CHARIOT (Cavalry Unit)
  • Defeating an enemy Unit provides Gold equal to 50% of its Combat Strength
  • Tier 2 variant available
  • Culture Tree Bonus: +2 Combat Strength in Rural Districts

GAL MESEDI (Commander Unit)
  • +1 Combat Strength vs Urban Districts for every Tradition in the Government

================================================================================
UNIQUE BUILDINGS & QUARTERS
================================================================================

HUWASI (Standing Stone)
  • +3 Happiness
  • +1 Food for each adjacent Resource
  • Can only be placed adjacent to Hills

GIŠKINTI (Smelter)
  • +2 Production
  • +2 Happiness to each Mined Resource assigned to the city
  • Can only be placed adjacent to Hills

KUNTARRA (Unique Quarter)
  • Created by constructing Huwasi and Giškinti in the same District
  • +10% Gold in this Settlement

================================================================================
WONDER
================================================================================

YAZILIKAYA
  • Constructing a Building or Wonder in this city grants Happiness equal to 
    25% of its Production cost
  • Must be built on a Rough Tile adjacent to an Iron Resource
  • Receives +30% Production boost from Civilization Ability

================================================================================
TRADITIONS
================================================================================

PANKUŠ
  • Towns have +1 Happiness per assigned resource
  • Cities have +3 Happiness per assigned resource
  • Available in Antiquity, Exploration, and Modern ages

RITUAL PITS
  • Clay Pits, Mines, and Quarries give +2 Culture
  • Clay Pits, Mines, and Quarries give -1 Production

GIFTS OF THE STORM GOD
  • +2 Happiness per Unassigned Resource

OATH OF LOYALTY
  • +1 Happiness per Unit packed in a commander

================================================================================
CIVICS ABILITIES
================================================================================

LABARNA
  • +1% Production per Bonus Resource assigned to a Settlement

EDICT OF TELEPINU
  • +5% Wonder production from Iron Resources

PERUNA HATRĀI (Rock Writing)
  • +1 Culture Adjacency with Hills
  • +1 Settlement Limit

SETTLEMENT LIMIT INCREASES
  • Available through various civic nodes for expanded empire growth

================================================================================
KEY STRENGTHS
================================================================================

1. RESOURCE-FOCUSED ECONOMY
   - Resource gathering and management provides Gold, Food, and Happiness
   - Unique buildings (Huwasi, Giškinti) enhance resource yields

2. MILITARY PROWESS
   - Golden units excel in specific terrain types (urban/rural)
   - Gold generation from defeating enemies creates self-sustaining military

3. SETTLEMENT EXPANSION
   - Multiple tradition and civic bonuses increase settlement limits
   - Happiness generation supports larger empires

4. PRODUCTION & CONSTRUCTION
   - Bonuses toward Wonder construction (Yazilikaya)
   - Resource-based Production increases

5. HAPPINESS GENERATION
   - Multiple traditions provide happiness from resources
   - Buildings generate happiness from construction completion
